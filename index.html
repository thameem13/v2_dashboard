What the signal was actually worth as a contract. Every graded candle buys the POC strike, next-day expiry, at the quoted ask; the session is then walked forward through the 5-minute chain snapshots and the trade exits on the first touch of the target or the stop, sold at the bid. Anything that reaches neither is closed at the end-of-day mark and shown as TP (EOD) or SL (EOD) by the sign of that exit - no trade is left open.
Exits are same-session only - the feed snapshots just the next-day chain, so a contract bought today is never quoted again tomorrow and an overnight hold cannot be measured at all. Premium history begins 2026-10-02.
Volume, C/P, RVOL and $ traded are whole-chain, for that candle, read from the same source as the signal modal and the Option Volume tab, so the three always agree. POC vol is the one exception - it is the traded contract's own volume, which is the liquidity you would actually have to fill.
